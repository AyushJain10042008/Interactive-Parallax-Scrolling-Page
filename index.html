Distant things move slowly.

Clouds far away drift past gently. The ones close to you rush by. That difference in speed is what makes a flat page feel deep.

Nearer stars move faster.

Tiny stars barely shift as you scroll, while the bright ones glide past. The planet and the moon each travel at their own pace too.

Three speeds, one illusion.

Far layers

The sun, the distant mountains and the tiniest stars. They move the least, so they look far away.

Middle layers

Mist, clouds and planets. They keep close to your scroll speed, so they sit in the middle ground.

Near layers

Foreground hills, bright stars and the rocket. They move fastest, so they look close enough to touch.

You made it.

Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No libraries and no images, just layers moving at different speeds.
